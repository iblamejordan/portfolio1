HUMAN ACTIVITY RECOGNITION M / 001
THE IDEA

Can a smartphone understand human movement?

MOTIONIQ explores how motion measurements collected by a smartphone can be used with machine learning to recognise everyday human activities. It uses the UCI Human Activity Recognition (HAR) dataset as its foundation.

DOMAINHuman Activity Recognition
DATAUCI HAR dataset
APPROACHSupervised classification
EXPERIMENTRandom Forest
